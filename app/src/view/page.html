          talk is on the yard. in chrome your voice goes to the browser's speech service to become text; nothing else leaves the page. to teach a new word, say it, wait for whatever the dog does, then praise it.

          associationsrw + pearce kaye hall

          rows are cues, columns are acts. cells show net strength, excitation minus a separate inhibition that fades faster, so an extinguished cue partly comes back after a break. the attn column is each cue's associability: it rises after surprises and falls as the outcome becomes predictable. credit fades with delay, so a slow treat can reward whatever the dog is doing when it lands. if nothing is strong the dog offers tricks it was rewarded for before; teach a new word by praising whatever it does.

                  stackall local

                  body: the owner's dog model, drawn by three.js on webgpu with a webgl2 fallback. two motion sources share the rig, a central pattern generator (walk, pace, trot, canter, gallop picked by froude number, raibert foot placement, two bone ik) and the neural gait (paws pinned by predicted contacts, retargeted as paw targets). switches between them are inertialized so nothing snaps. springs drive posture, gaze, ears and a tail with eight wag modes.

                  brain: each act scores by how much it would reduce the dog's needs (keramati and gutkin 2011). the choice is sampled from a softmax with a commitment bonus; urgent acts start after a short heavy tailed latency while the head turns first. sleep pressure and a two peak body clock run onset, nrem, rem and wake. learning is rescorla wagner with pearce kaye hall associability, a faster fading inhibition and eligibility traces.

                  senses: itti koch saliency on the dog eye frame with top down drive bias and inhibition of return, looming startle from time to contact, per dog sound direction error, a scent plume sampled in sniff bouts and tracked upwind. yolov8n labels the eye feed when you turn it on.

                  human: moheeb with retargeted quaternius clips (cc0), a gaze model, velocity springs, planted feet and a phased throw. his day runs on needs and an hourly routine; the top three options are drawn at random, weighted.

                  cortex: gemma 4 e2b or lfm2-vl 450m on webgpu sets goals on events, from a memory stream ranked by recency, importance and relevance (park 2023). reflexes and timing stay in code.

                  packages: @hackbuild/gait, @hackbuild/motion-net, @hackbuild/senses, @hackbuild/drives, @hackbuild/cameras, @hackbuild/tunables, all mit. physics is rapier.
