rows are cues, columns are actions. cells show net strength, excitation minus a separate inhibition that fades faster, so an extinguished cue partly comes back after a break. the attn column is each cue's associability: it rises after surprises and falls as the outcome becomes predictable. credit decays with delay (eligibility trace), so a slow treat can reward whatever the dog is doing when it lands. if nothing is strong the dog offers tricks it was rewarded for before; teach a new word by praising whatever it does.

body: your meshy glb, drawn by three.js webgpurenderer with a webgl2 fallback. two motion sources share the rig: a central pattern generator (walk, pace, trot, canter, gallop picked by froude number with hysteresis, raibert foot placement, two bone ik) and a neural gait from dog motion capture (ai4animationpy codebook matching, retargeted as paw targets scaled by leg length and locked by predicted contacts, plus body pitch, roll and height). switches between them, and between postures, are inertialized (holden) so nothing snaps.

on top: critically damped springs for posture and gaze, a tail chain driven by eight discrete wag modes with an owner side bias (ren 2022, quaranta 2007), spring ears kicked by head motion, centripetal lean and fore and hind load shift, a mass scaled shake off (dickerson 2012), breathing jittered per cycle, pink noise drift so a still dog is never frozen, and paw paddling in rem.

brain: drives reward by how much they reduce internal deficits (keramati and gutkin 2011). the choice is sampled from a softmax with a commitment bonus, urgent options start after an ex-gaussian latency while the head turns first, and quiet changes wait for the current act to finish. sleep pressure plus a two peak body clock run onset, nrem, rem and wake. play bows need eye contact, a scolding brings the appeasement set keyed to his posture (horowitz 2009), mutual gaze builds a bond (maclean and hare 2015), pointing is followed by gesture type and bond (clark 2019), and exploring is bolder with him in view. learning keeps rescorla-wagner with pearce-kaye-hall associability, a separate inhibitory weight that fades faster, eligibility traces so a late treat can teach the wrong thing, and free operant offers.

senses: itti-koch saliency on the dog eye frame (intensity, blue yellow, edges, motion weighted highest) with top down drive bias and inhibition of return kept in world directions; looming startle from time to contact; per dog sound direction error (guerineau 2022); acuity that falls from bright to dim light (lind 2017); a puff plume with whiffs and blanks plus decaying ground trails, sampled only on sniffs at 4 to 7 Hz in bouts (craven 2010), tracked by surging upwind and casting crosswind (celani 2014). yolov8n (agpl 3.0 weights, fetched when you turn it on under sense) can label the eye feed.

human: moheeb with retargeted quaternius ual clips (cc0). his gaze follows saccade statistics (eyes alive), smartbody gaze ratios and stahl head recruitment; root motion uses velocity springs and the walking curvature law; planted feet lock; reaches follow minimum jerk; the throw is a phased kinematic chain with jitter; he breathes, sways, shows mood in his posture, approaches the dog from the side, and points.

cortex: gemma 4 e2b or lfm2-vl 450m on webgpu sets goals on events, using a memory stream ranked by recency, importance and relevance (park 2023). reflexes and timing stay in js.

every number above is live in the tune tab, including seven temperament traits (jones and gosling 2005). each layer can be switched off to compare against the old canned build.
